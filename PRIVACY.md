# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-10  
**Project:** Cronos-CTC (`theworker02/ChronosCTC`)

## Summary

Cronos-CTC is an **open-source software project**. The libraries and CLI distributed from this repository and from [crates.io](https://crates.io) are designed to run **locally on your machine**.

**We do not operate a SaaS backend that collects personal data from the runtime.**

## What this software does *not* do

By default, Cronos-CTC crates and the `cronos-ctc` binary:

- do **not** phone home
- do **not** upload worldline state, telemetry, or personal information
- do **not** require an account
- do **not** embed advertising or trackers

## Data processed locally

When you run the software, all chronal state (DAGs, residuals, holographic boundaries, horizon checkpoints) remains on the host unless **you** choose to export, commit, or transmit it.

Configuration files such as `configs/runtime.toml` may contain operational parameters (tolerances, device preferences). They are not transmitted by the project.
